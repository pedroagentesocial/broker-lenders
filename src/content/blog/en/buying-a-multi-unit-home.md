Living in one unit and renting out the others is one of the oldest ways to buy a home you couldn't otherwise afford. Lenders treat it differently from buying a rental. As long as you'll live there, a two- to four-unit property can be financed as your **primary residence**, with a far smaller down payment than an investment property, and the rent from the other units can help you qualify.

A high-balance loan is a conventional loan above the standard limit but within the higher limit of an expensive county. If you'd move out and rent every unit instead, it's an investment property, and the conventional minimum jumps to 25% down on two to four units.

## How the rent helps you qualify

The lender doesn't count the full rent. On a purchase, the appraiser estimates market rent for each unit on a small residential income property report, and the lender also looks at any lease that transfers to you.

<div class="bl-checks">
  <div class="bl-check"><span class="bl-check-icon">✓</span><span><b>Conventional counts 75%</b> Fannie Mae uses 75% of the monthly gross rent, not the full amount.</span></div>
  <div class="bl-check is-warn"><span class="bl-check-icon">!</span><span><b>Landlord experience matters on conventional</b> With at least 12 months of experience managing rental property, the rental income can count in full. With less, it can only help cover the new house payment, not add to your income.</span></div>
  <div class="bl-check"><span class="bl-check-icon">✓</span><span><b>FHA counts 75% of the lower number</b> FHA uses 75% of the lower of the appraiser's market rent or the lease, and adds it to your income. It can't be used to reduce your mortgage payment instead.</span></div>
</div>

So if the other three units of a fourplex are leased for $1,500 each, the lender starts from $3,375 a month, not $4,500.

## FHA's extra test for three and four units

On a three- or four-unit building, FHA also checks that the property can carry itself. The appraiser's market rent for **all** the units, including the one you'll live in, minus the larger of the appraiser's vacancy and maintenance estimate or 25%, has to be at least as much as the full mortgage payment with taxes and insurance.

## Plan for reserves

More units means more can go wrong, and lenders want cash left over after closing. On a conventional loan approved through Fannie Mae's automated system, a two- to four-unit primary residence needs **six months** of reserves. FHA requires **three months** of the full payment on three- and four-unit homes.
