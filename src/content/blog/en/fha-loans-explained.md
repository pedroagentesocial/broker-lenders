FHA loans have a reputation as the "easy" mortgage. That's half right. The Federal Housing Administration doesn't lend the money. It insures the loan, which lets approved lenders take on buyers with smaller down payments and lower credit scores than a conventional loan usually allows. In exchange, you pay for that insurance, and on most FHA loans you pay it for as long as you keep the loan.

Everything below comes from HUD's Handbook 4000.1, the rulebook every FHA lender follows, in its August 2026 version. Lenders can add stricter rules of their own on top.

## The down payment depends on your credit score

FHA calls the down payment your **Minimum Required Investment**, and it's at least 3.5% of the home's value. Your credit score decides whether 3.5% is enough:

Lenders can set their own minimum above 500, so a score in the 500s narrows your choices even though the program allows it.

## What the mortgage insurance actually costs

FHA charges two premiums. The **upfront premium** is 1.75% of the loan, and it can be added to the loan instead of paid in cash. The **annual premium** is billed monthly with your payment. For a loan longer than 15 years and up to $726,200, it works like this:

## How big a loan FHA will insure

FHA sets a maximum loan by county. For case numbers assigned in 2026, the limit for a single-family home runs from a **floor of $541,287** in lower-cost areas to a **ceiling of $1,249,125** in the most expensive ones. Homes with two to four units have higher limits. You can look up your county on HUD's FHA Mortgage Limits page.

## How much payment you can carry

Most FHA loans run through FHA's automated scorecard, which weighs your whole file at once. When a loan is underwritten by hand, the handbook sets the ratios. With a score of 580 or higher and no compensating factors, the limit is 31% of your income for the house payment and 43% for all debts. With strong compensating factors, such as verified savings or a small jump from your current rent, it can go as high as 40% and 50%. Below 580, or with no score, the limit stays at 31/43.
